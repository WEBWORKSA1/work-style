The Coordinator

You keep people, plans and deadlines moving — the team runs smoother with you in it.

Quick sprintsCollaborativePlannedHybrid

Coordinators are the connective tissue of a team. They switch between conversations easily, love a clear plan and make sure everyone knows what's happening next. They're energized by people and by order at the same time.

Strengths

  • Keeps projects on schedule
  • Communicates clearly and often
  • Resolves blockers and conflicts early
  • Good at running meetings and rituals

Watch-outs

  • Own deep work gets crowded out
  • Can over-communicate or over-meet
  • Frustrated by teammates who ignore the plan

Where you work best

An office or hybrid setup with good meeting rooms — or a remote team with strong async tools.

A day that fits the Coordinator

  1. 08:30 Review project boards and deadlines
  2. 09:00 Stand-up or async check-in
  3. 09:30 Short meetings and quick decisions
  4. 13:00 One protected hour for your own work
  5. 14:00 Stakeholder updates
  6. 16:30 Prepare tomorrow's agenda

Tools and habits that help

  • Project boards
  • Shared calendars
  • Meeting templates
  • Status-update templates

Productivity methods to try

Kanban, Eisenhower matrix, Time blocking. Read how each works in 9 productivity methods.

Works well with
