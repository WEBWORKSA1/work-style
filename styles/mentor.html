The Mentor

You think deeply and plan carefully — and you do it best alongside other people.

Deep focusCollaborativePlannedHybrid

Mentors combine depth with connection. They prefer fewer, longer conversations to many short ones, and they like structured collaboration: a pairing session with an agenda, a design review with prepared questions, a planned one-to-one. They grow teams as well as output.

Strengths

  • Raises the skill level of everyone around them
  • Runs focused, well-prepared meetings
  • Builds trust and long-term working relationships
  • Good at turning expertise into documentation and process

Watch-outs

  • Calendar fills with other people's needs
  • Can take on too much coaching and too little doing
  • Unstructured brainstorming feels like wasted time

Where you work best

A hybrid setup: office days for pairing and reviews, home days for deep preparation and writing.

A day that fits the Mentor

  1. 08:30 Prepare for the day's conversations
  2. 09:00–11:00 Deep work on your own deliverable
  3. 11:00–12:30 Pairing or one-to-ones with an agenda
  4. 14:00–15:30 Review and feedback block
  5. 16:00 Write up decisions and next steps

Tools and habits that help

  • Shared agendas for every meeting
  • A team wiki or handbook
  • Office hours instead of ad-hoc interruptions
  • A calendar with protected focus blocks

Productivity methods to try

Time blocking, Office hours, Weekly review. Read how each works in 9 productivity methods.

Works well with

The Tinkerer, whose experiments benefit from a Mentor's structure.
