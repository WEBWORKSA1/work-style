The Catalyst

You bring energy, ideas and momentum — and you think best in motion, with people.

Quick sprintsCollaborativeFlexibleOffice-first

Catalysts spark things. They're quick, social and spontaneous, and they're often the person who gets a stuck project moving again. They love brainstorms, launches and new ventures, and they get bored by repetition.

Strengths

  • Builds energy and buy-in quickly
  • Great at pitching, selling and starting things
  • Adapts on the fly
  • Builds wide networks

Watch-outs

  • Follow-through on routine work
  • Can start more than the team can finish
  • Long solo work feels draining

Where you work best

A lively office, coworking space or client-facing role, with a partner who handles the details.

A day that fits the Catalyst

  1. 09:00 Quick team energizer or check-in
  2. 09:30 Calls, pitches and conversations
  3. 12:00 Lunch with someone new
  4. 13:30 A 60-minute sprint on one deliverable
  5. 15:00 Brainstorm or workshop
  6. 16:30 Hand off details to the right owner

Tools and habits that help

  • Voice memos
  • Shared task boards
  • Calendar booking links
  • A finishing partner or assistant

Productivity methods to try

Eat the frog, Pair working, Kanban. Read how each works in 9 productivity methods.

Works well with

The Sprinter, who turns sparks into shipped work.
