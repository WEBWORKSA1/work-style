Contests and prizes

Show us how you work. Every month we feature the best entries and send prizes to the winners.

Show Your Setup

Send one photo of your workspace — home office, kitchen table, van, coworking desk — and 100–300 words on why it works for your style.

Monthly prize: a featured profile plus a $100 gift card or sponsor-donated gear.

My Work Style Story

Tell us, in 300–800 words, about a time changing how you work changed your results. Winning stories are published with your byline.

Quarterly prize: $250 and a published feature.

Best Hybrid Teams

Nominate a team (yours or someone else's) that's cracked remote or hybrid work. Winners get a profile and a digital badge.

Yearly award: recognition and a free team workshop.

Enter a contest

Rules in plain language

  1. Open to people aged 18 or over, where contests are legal. No purchase or payment necessary.
  2. One entry per person per contest per period.
  3. Entries must be your own original work and must not show other people without their permission, or private or confidential information.
  4. By entering, you allow Work.Style to publish your entry, name and country on this site and its social channels. You keep ownership of your work.
  5. Winners are chosen by the Work.Style editorial team on originality, usefulness to readers and fit with the theme. Decisions are final.
  6. Winners are contacted by email and must reply within 14 days, or a runner-up is chosen.
  7. Prizes can't be exchanged. Winners are responsible for any taxes on prizes.
  8. We may change or cancel a contest if needed; any change will be posted here.
