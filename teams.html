Fix team friction before it starts.

Most team conflict isn't about effort or skill — it's about style. A Work.Style team report maps everyone on one page and turns it into a working rhythm the whole team can live with.

What you get

  • Team map: every member on the three work-style axes, on one page
  • Friction report: where your team's styles clash, with examples and fixes
  • Rhythm plan: meeting cadence, focus time and async norms tuned to your team
  • Hybrid schedule: recommended anchor days and office-day agenda
  • Pairing guide: which teammates complement each other
  • Optional workshop: a 90-minute facilitated session, live or remote

Questions teams ask

Is this a personality test?

No. It measures work preferences — how you like to focus, collaborate and plan — not personality, ability or mental health.

Can we use it for hiring or performance reviews?

No, and we won't support that use. It isn't designed or validated for selection decisions. Use it to improve how a team works together.

Who sees individual answers?

Only the summary result for each person goes into the team map, and each person sees their own report. Raw answers are not shared with managers.

Do you work with remote teams in other time zones?

Yes. Everything can be done online, and workshops can be run in two sessions for teams spread across time zones.

We're a small team. Is it worth it?

Small teams often get the most out of it, because one mismatch affects everyone. Teams of three and up are welcome.
