How to use it

Meetings are where many teams lose the most time without noticing. The calculator multiplies the number of people by their hourly cost and the meeting length, then by how often the meeting happens. Use it before adding a recurring meeting to your calendar, or during a quarterly calendar audit. Pair it with our guide to meeting hygiene.
