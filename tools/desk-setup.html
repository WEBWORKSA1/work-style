How to use it

Most aches from desk work come from a handful of measurements: seat, desk and screen heights. The results are starting points based on average body proportions; adjust until your elbows are at about 90 degrees, your feet are flat and your neck is neutral.

Results are estimates for planning only. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
