How to use it

The timer runs entirely in your browser. Choose the classic Pomodoro rhythm for task-heavy days, 50/10 for writing and analysis, or 90/20 for deep work. Keep this tab open; a short tone plays when each session ends. Learn which rhythm suits you in 9 productivity methods.
